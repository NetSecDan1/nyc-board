    Know before you go

    Field notes

    Saving money

    • Sunset views: Gantry Plaza in Queens is free and the Roosevelt Island Tram costs a subway fare. Top of the Rock runs about $45+ a person.
    • Split the big stuff. One Katz's pastrami sandwich feeds two.
    • Chinatown bites are mostly a few dollars each, and the Bryant Park rink is free to skate.

    Chinatown grazing

    • Bring cash in small bills. Many bakeries and dumpling counters are cash only.
    • Order one of each and share, so the group can try far more.
    • The route is a loop on the map: south, Canal St, north, Little Italy, then back south.

    Canal Street knockoffs

    The "fake bag" strip is Canal Street between roughly Broadway and Mott St, right where Chinatown starts.

    • Sellers whisper "bags, watches" and show a photo catalog, then walk you to a back room or van. Stay together and don't follow anyone alone.
    • Selling counterfeits is illegal and police do sweeps, so stalls come and go.
    • Cash, small bills, haggle hard. Quality is hit or miss.

    Weather & light

    Mid-December runs about 30–42°F, often windy downtown. It's dark by about 4:30 PM, which is why lights and the tree are planned for late afternoon. Waterproof shoes beat cute ones; you'll walk 6–10 miles a day.

    Subway

    Tap a phone or contactless card at the turnstile (OMNY); no MetroCard needed. Fares cap after enough rides in a week. The hotel is a short walk from 47–50 Sts–Rockefeller Ctr (B D F M), 49 St (N R W) and 50 St (1, C E).

    Things that close

    • Most Broadway shows are dark on Mondays, so the show is on Tuesday.
    • The 9/11 Museum normally closes Tuesdays, so downtown is Wednesday. The outdoor Memorial pools are free and open daily.
    • The Rockefeller tree is lit from Dec 2 and stays up into January.
